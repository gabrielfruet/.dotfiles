# Social post (LinkedIn)

```text
max_lines: none
max_words: none
rewrite: inline
```

A post read by people outside the team. It reads as one person thinking out loud,
so the sentences connect.

## Rules

- Full sentences that lead into each other. Join related facts with "and",
  "so", "because" instead of stacking them as separate lines.
- No runs of short lines or fragments. One short sentence for emphasis is fine;
  two in a row is a drumbeat.
- No number punchlines. Put figures inside a sentence that says what they mean.
- Check the draft against `patterns.md` ("Manufactured punchlines") before
  shipping.

## Allowed here

```text
fragments:       banned
em dashes:       avoid
headers & bold:  banned
```

## Example

Bad:

> 1598 tensors. About 8 rounds. One fused block.

Good:

> It took about eight rounds to get all 1598 tensors into one fused block.
