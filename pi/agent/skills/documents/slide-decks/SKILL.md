# Slide decks

Content rules. The pptx skill owns the file mechanics.

## Screenshots

Keep the native aspect ratio. Never crop a screenshot to make its detail larger
— a 16:9 capture cut to 4:3 reads as a broken image, not a zoom. Scale the whole
frame down to fit the placeholder instead. When a crop is unavoidable, land it on
16:9, 4:3 or 1:1, and crop every screenshot on the slide to the same ratio.

Three screenshots across a slide leave each one about 3 inches wide. Detail that
small is unreadable at any ratio — cut to two, or give the one that matters a
full slide.

## Copy

Name the action and the product, never the widget the user clicks. "Train a new
model in LightlyStudio", not "train a new model from a form". A form, a dialog
and a panel are implementation; the reader cares what they get.
